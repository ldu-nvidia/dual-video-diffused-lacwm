# Final compact object/contact distribution Stage-0 protocol

Date frozen: 2026-08-08

Status: **prospective for the remaining 31 episodes; their RGB/state targets
have never been opened**

## Sequential question and evidence boundary

The fresh deterministic slot study stopped even though oracle slots were
relevant. A conditional mean can nevertheless fail when future interaction is
multimodal. This final interaction-branch study asks whether exact planned
action improves a compact **conditional distribution** over object/contact
outcomes.

The deterministic fit256 remains model-fitting data. Its opened fresh64 score
set is demoted permanently to bandwidth calibration and cannot count as
evidence here. All 31 D405 episodes left unused by that registration become
the single final score set. They contain 8/8/8/7 episodes in the four original
planned-motion strata. Registration must seal their identities, within-stratum
shuffle donors, calibrated model, and source commit before any remaining RGB
pixel or measured state array is indexed. Validation and protected test remain
unsupported.

## Compact stable target

Apply the already frozen K=4 causal slot extractor without modification. Map
the eight future slot transitions to seven slot-permutation-invariant scalars:

1. `log1p(1000 * total positive+negative event mass)`;
2. event-mass-weighted normalized future time;
3. event-mass-weighted centroid x;
4. event-mass-weighted centroid y;
5. event-mass-weighted signed horizontal motion;
6. event-mass-weighted signed vertical motion;
7. maximum observed contact-proximity score.

A future with zero event mass maps exactly to seven zeros. Apply the analogous
summary to the four observed history transitions. Standardize both from
fit256 only. This fixed state represents occurrence, timing, location, motion,
and contact while avoiding persistent-slot permutation and 448-dimensional
conditional-density estimation.

## Fixed equal-K conditional distribution

Every arm uses exactly K=32 equal-weight Gaussian components. Component means
are the compact targets of the 32 nearest fit256 episodes. All components in
an arm share calibrated isotropic variance in fit-standardized target space.

- History-only neighbor distance is mean squared compact-history distance.
- Action-conditioned distance adds mean squared distance in fit-standardized
  PCA8 of planned actions `[4:12,5,14]` with fixed weight 1.0.
- Aligned, shuffled, and action-stratum arms all use the same action-conditioned
  model and bandwidth; only the query action changes.
- Episode-shuffled action rotates the final episodes within their original
  motion stratum.
- The action-stratum query uses the fit256 mean action-PCA coordinate for the
  final episode's original motion stratum.

No neural model, mixture component count, target coordinate, or action weight
can be selected using the final31.

## Calibration64 only

For history and action models separately, select one scalar standard deviation
from `{0.20,0.30,0.45,0.65,0.90,1.25,1.75}` using only opened calibration64.
Prefer candidates whose pooled marginal central-80% coverage is 70--90% and
central-95% coverage is 87--100%; among them minimize mean held-out NLL. If no
candidate meets both, minimize total absolute coverage deviation from 80% and
95%, then NLL, then sigma. Store the full grid and selected sigmas in sealed
registration. Calibration scores are not evidence for the final claim.

## Proper final scores and controls

Score each final episode with four lower-is-better proper rules:

1. held-out mixture negative log likelihood in nats per target dimension;
2. multivariate energy score using 256 fixed common-random-number samples;
3. exact mean marginal Gaussian-mixture CRPS;
4. weighted interval score using the median and central 80%/95% intervals.

For each score, compare aligned against history-only, within-stratum shuffled,
and action-stratum centroid: 12 mandatory contrasts. Use one common
20,000-draw final-episode bootstrap with seed 20260816 and one-sided
Bonferroni confidence `1 - 0.05/12 = 99.5833%`.

Every NLL contrast must improve by at least 0.05 nats per dimension, have a
strictly positive simultaneous absolute lower bound, and favor at least 60%
of final episodes. Every energy/CRPS/WIS contrast must improve by at least 5%,
have a strictly positive simultaneous relative lower bound, and favor at least
60% of episodes.

Aligned marginal coverage must also lie in 70--90% for the central-80%
interval and 87--100% for the central-95% interval. At least half of final
targets must be nonzero and nonzero targets must occur in all four motion
strata.

Only if all 12 proper-score effects and every coverage/salience check pass may
the decision be `GO_FOR_STOCHASTIC_OBJECT_RESIDUAL_SCREEN`. Any failure yields
`CLOSE_INTERACTION_BRANCH`. This Stage-0 run cannot launch Wan or claim video
quality, speed, or control benefit.

## Claim boundary

This is a one-shot 31-episode train-population test of a deliberately simple
equal-weight conditional mixture. The small final set limits power under the
strict familywise gate. Passing would justify only a separate stochastic
residual-generator screen; failure closes this interaction proxy branch, not
all possible semantic/physical world-state modeling.
